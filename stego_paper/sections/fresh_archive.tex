\subsection{Fresh archive reconstruction and sender-only changes}
\label{sec:fresh-archive}
\input{../common/data/fresh_archive_values.tex}

The G5 writing variant is defined in Section~\ref{sec:prompt-ablation}; here it is tested on complete messages rather than individual candidate comments.

Prepared-context recovery establishes how the receiver reads carriers when its lists are available. The archive experiments examine the next dependency: obtaining compatible public inputs independently. Within that setting, the sender-policy comparisons ask whether additional attempts or shorter accepted replies change complete-message delivery and its cost.

These experiments evaluate a local public-observation simulation of the existing deterministic thread-only variant. Sender and receiver independently fetch the archived post and up to 100 earliest comments, then reconstruct the extractive codebook. The receiver derives the comment cutoff from the existing agreed timing window and orders locally simulated carriers by visible timestamps and IDs. A separate archive driver performs this filtering and reference construction; the full production receiver does not yet implement public cutoff or discovery. Decoding does not use sender codebooks, index maps, expected hashes, payload lengths or private carrier order. Round 4 reads only the timing value from a manifest that also contains private analyst metadata; those values are accessible but unused. Round 5 and 6 processes instead load separate minimal public configuration and observation files, without loading the private artifacts. This is an input boundary, not an operating-system sandbox. This adds no sender-to-receiver shared parameter and does not test actual Reddit publication, discovery, full web/LLM reconstruction or security.

Round 4 assigned six posts to the existing anchored and G5 writers, using local Qwen 3.5 9B and two writer attempts per frame. Primary exact recovery was \FreshFourAnchoredExact/\FreshAssigned{} versus \FreshFourGfiveExact/\FreshAssigned{}. A receiver-only archive-fetch retry recovered a fifth anchored message without regenerating text; it does not change the primary count. Acquisition and generation failures remain in the assigned denominators. This bounded negative result does not establish that G5 fails universally.

Rounds 5 and 6 compare three sender policies with unchanged prompts, codebook-construction algorithm and decoder. Each source has its independently rebuilt post-specific list. Baseline2 uses the initial two-attempt prefix of a fresh generation stream and stops at the first frame unavailable within that budget. Baseline7 permits seven attempts, the existing default. Concise7 uses that same accepted pool, keeps baseline eligible, and chooses the fewest-word strictly accepted reply whose pinned CPU MiniLM parent cosine is no lower than baseline's. Ties prefer higher cosine, then original candidate order. Parent cosine is a proxy, not a naturalness judgment.

Source selection for both panels is described in Section~\ref{sec:datasets}. Before generation, we froze the post IDs, settings, source and model identities, payloads, and quality-selection rules. Failed cases were not replaced. Each assigned eight-byte printable ASCII message occupies \FreshStorageBits{} storage bits; that size is not a claim of uniform random entropy. The private derivation salt and expected bytes never enter the receiver.

There are six paired source-post clusters per panel, not 18 independent trials. The two-attempt baseline is a prefix counterfactual with its actual prefix cost, including rejected calls at its stopping frame. Both seven-attempt policies are charged the full common generation work, paid once in the experiment; each policy has a separate independently fetching receiver. All assignments fit planning; the incomplete sequences exhausted generation, rather than failing capacity or disagreeing on public codebooks. Both untouched AskScience messages failed generation; the larger budget rescues one technology and one news message. It also rescues two development messages. Receiver call counts are \FreshDevTwoReceiverCalls/\FreshDevSevenReceiverCalls/\FreshDevConciseReceiverCalls{} in development and \FreshHeldTwoReceiverCalls/\FreshHeldSevenReceiverCalls/\FreshHeldConciseReceiverCalls{} on untouched posts, in policy order. Selection adds no generation LLM calls but requires CPU embedding and selection work. Concise selection preserves the observed exact counts and reduces all-attempt words by \FreshDevSaving\% in development and \FreshHeldSaving\% on untouched posts. Nominal selection capacity per frame is unchanged. No silent payload mismatch was observed; these small panels do not establish its future probability.

The unchanged blinded pairwise writing task uses post and actual parent context, selecting the first two changed frames per post or fewer, with both candidate orders. GPT-OSS 20B at temperature zero assessed \FreshDevPairs{} development pairs through \FreshDevOrders{} presentations: both orders preferred baseline in \FreshDevBaselineWins{}, concise in \FreshDevConciseWins{}, tied in \FreshDevTies{}, and disagreed in \FreshDevDiscordant{}. The untouched subset contains \FreshHeldPairs{} pairs from \FreshHeldQualityPosts{} posts, \FreshHeldOrders{} presentations, with \FreshHeldBaselineWins{} stable baseline preferences, \FreshHeldConciseWins{} concise preference and \FreshHeldDiscordant{} disagreement. All responses were valid; presentations are not independent samples. These results support neither a quality improvement nor demonstrated quality preservation. The judge can reward conversational anecdotes, including unverified first-person work experience; this is not factual validation. We retain anchored baseline7 as the working choice and treat concision as an optional experimental efficiency tradeoff.

Both language versions read the same generated values and SHA256-pinned evidence snapshot. These fresh observations do not retrospectively validate the older shared-state or audit-assisted campaigns. Bundle detectability, public discovery, full reconstruction and editing resistance remain untested.
