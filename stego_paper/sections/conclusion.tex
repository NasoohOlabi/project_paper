\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This research introduces a context-aware semantic steganography framework for threaded discussions that redefines the embedding domain as a \textit{selection channel}. By transitioning from token-level manipulation to encoding payload bits into reply targeting and Angle selection, the method aims to preserve contextual naturalness in the visible text while retaining black-box compatibility with LLM APIs.

The implementation includes a closed-loop verification mechanism that simulates decoding prior to publication and retries synthesis under stochastic generation. Our artifact-based evaluation reports naturalness and unigram divergence metrics on successful, receiver-validated samples, and characterizes the dominant failure modes when retries are exhausted. Future work will focus on improving end-to-end success rates on fresh real threads, expanding clean sample sizes, and performing dedicated steganalysis evaluations under explicit adversarial models.
