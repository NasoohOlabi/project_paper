\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This research introduces a context-aware semantic steganography framework for threaded discussions that redefines the embedding domain as a \textit{selection channel}. Payload bits select a reply target and an Angle, while an LLM realizes the selected choice as visible text. The design avoids token-level payload insertion and supports black-box LLM access, but its recoverable capacity is limited by the available comment and Angle states.

The paired benchmark attempted 627 samples with our method and obtained 554 successes; the baseline was invoked on 460 of these and 304 of its outputs were hide/reveal decode-verified. Across 100 post-clustered source posts, ZLG had higher recoverable capacity (16.00 versus 15.14 bits/comment) and lower GPT-2 perplexity (53.70 versus 68.15), whereas our method had a slightly higher exploratory lexical-quality index (99.36 versus 98.45) and longer replies. The project-side rows used audit-assisted compressed-bitstring recovery, so these results show a configuration trade-off rather than a blind end-to-end superiority claim. Future work should rerun blind decoding, reduce dependence on reused posts and exhausted search quotas, and test dedicated steganalysis and editing attacks.
