This paper proposes a black-box linguistic steganography workflow for threaded discussions that hides bits by choosing a reply target and a comment topic, then writing an ordinary comment.
The receiver recovers those choices from a supplied thread snapshot and shared operational parameters, without relying on any hidden markup.
The method does not use invisible characters.
To check that the generated comments read naturally, we scored them on several criteria with a separate language model---not the one used to generate them---against comments from an earlier baseline method.
That judge flagged our comments as machine-written less often than the baseline's and rated them higher on register-fit.
